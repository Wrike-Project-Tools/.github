## ⚙️ What Wrike Brings to Project Management Workflows

Wrike is a work management platform designed to help teams plan projects, coordinate tasks, and maintain visibility into ongoing work. Its configurable workspace supports different processes across departments and project types.

Task management features help teams break projects into smaller activities with assigned owners, due dates, priorities, and statuses. Clear task records make responsibilities easier to understand and help teams identify work that requires attention.

Project planning tools support the organisation of milestones, timelines, and deliverables. Depending on the available configuration, teams can review schedules through different views and understand how individual tasks contribute to broader project goals.

Task dependencies help represent relationships between activities where supported. Identifying prerequisite work can improve coordination and make scheduling conflicts easier to recognise before they affect later deliverables.

Team collaboration features provide places to share updates, discuss assignments, and maintain context around project decisions. Keeping discussions connected to relevant work items can reduce the need to search through separate communication channels.

Resource management capabilities help teams review workloads and coordinate people across projects. Capacity planning can be particularly useful when several initiatives compete for the same specialists, although accurate planning depends on reliable estimates and availability information.

Workflow features help standardise how work moves between stages. Teams can organise intake, review, approval, and completion processes, using supported rules and notifications to reduce repetitive administrative steps.

Dashboards and reporting tools help managers review project activity and operational indicators. Depending on the configuration, reports can provide visibility into deadlines, workload, progress, and other information useful for planning.

Portfolio management supports oversight across multiple projects where the relevant features are available. Teams can compare project status, identify dependencies, and review priorities in relation to broader organisational objectives.

Wrike can support specialised workflows for marketing, product development, professional services, event planning, and other team activities. Configurable workspaces allow organisations to adapt processes without requiring every department to follow an identical task structure.

Integrations can connect Wrike with compatible communication, file-sharing, development, and business applications. The available options depend on the integration, account permissions, subscription, and current product offering.

Access controls and workspace permissions help administrators manage who can view or modify project information. Regular permission reviews are important when teams change, external collaborators are involved, or projects contain sensitive business details.

Desktop and browser access options can help users work with project information in different environments. Application availability and supported features may vary by operating system, device, and current product configuration.

Wrike's feature availability depends on the selected subscription and workspace settings. Organisations should review current plan limits, reporting capabilities, integrations, automation options, and resource-management functions before designing critical workflows around specific features.

Effective project management also depends on consistent task updates, realistic deadlines, clear ownership, and regular reviews. Wrike provides tools to organise these practices, while teams remain responsible for maintaining accurate information and making appropriate project decisions.

## 🚀 Starting a Wrike Project Session

**Prerequisites:** Internet access, a supported browser or compatible application, and authorised access to a Wrike workspace.

1. **Define the Project:** Identify the desired outcome, major deliverables, stakeholders, and target deadlines.
2. **Open the Access Page:** Select the GET — Wrike button above to continue to the designated access page and review the available instructions.
3. **Access Your Workspace:** Open Wrike through the appropriate access route and sign in with your authorised account.
4. **Create or Select a Project:** Choose a suitable workspace or project structure and organise the initial tasks, owners, and deadlines.
5. **Configure the Workflow:** Set priorities, review dependencies, organise team access, and configure supported notifications or automation.
6. **Review Project Progress:** Update task statuses, inspect upcoming deadlines, and use available dashboards or reports to evaluate the current workload.
